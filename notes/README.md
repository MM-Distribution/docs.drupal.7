# Notes (not part of the drupal.org archive)

This folder holds our own notes, corrections, considerations and examples about
the archived Drupal 7 docs. Nothing here is from drupal.org, and nothing here is
ever mixed into `raw/` or `md/`.

Suggested layout: mirror the page you are annotating, e.g. notes about
`md/docs/7/api/database-api/dynamic-queries/joins.md` go in
`notes/docs/7/api/database-api/dynamic-queries/joins.md`.
