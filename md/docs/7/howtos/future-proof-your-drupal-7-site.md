# Future-proof your Drupal 7 site

Drupal 8 is coming! But what decisions can you make now on your current or new Drupal 7 sites to make transitioning to Drupal 8 easier?

This page is based on David Reid's presentation [DrupalCon Austin 2014: FUTURE-PROOF YOUR DRUPAL 7 SITE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z3_MqGLjqkA). Dave covers new features available in Drupal 8, and how you can use those new features now in Drupal 7. Dave Reid also did [a similar presentation at DrupalCon Amsterdam 2014](https://amsterdam2014.drupal.org/session/future-proof-your-drupal-7-site).
