# Upgrade procedure (major version change)

The following pages explain upgrading from Drupal 6 to Drupal 7. For updating within major versions (a version of Drupal 7 to a later version of Drupal 7), [see the documentation on updating](https://www.drupal.org/node/1223018).

For upgrading from Drupal 6 to Drupal 8 or from Drupal 7 to Drupal 8, see [Upgrade to Drupal 8 guide](https://www.drupal.org/docs/8/upgrade).
